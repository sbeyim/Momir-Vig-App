# Momir-Vig-App
Background:
Momir-Vig is a format or way to play Magic: The Gathering that is only available to play for a limited time on Wizards of the Coast's online platform called Magic: The Gathering Arena. This Program allows for people to continue to enjoy this fun format ouside of those limited times.

How does the game work?
In Magic: The Gathering, you build up a currency, called mana, that is normally used to play creatures or spells that help you win the game. Traditionally, you must balance your creation of this resource as well as the other cards in your deck that help you beat your opponents. This format of Momir-Vig flips this concept on its head. Your deck is made of only currency and you spend that each turn to find a random creature from the existance of almost 20,000 creatures. This adds loads of randomness, fun, and flexibilty to an already amazing game.

What Does This Program Do?
This program assists in the part of the game that WoTC's online platform normally solves. If you wanted to play this format with your friend, you would need to find a way to randomly pick a creature from the whole library of existance. This program takes an input of the cost that you paid for a creature and connects to the API of a database with all the MTG cards and randomly selects one for you to create.
