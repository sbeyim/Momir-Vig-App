How to Play Momir Vig

Momir Vig is a Magic: The Gathering format where every player's deck is only basic lands, and creatures come from one repeatable ability. This site plays the part of the "random creature" step for you when playing in person.

Setup

  • Each player builds a deck of basic lands only (60 cards is typical).
  • Each player uses a Momir Vig, Simic Visionary Vanguard card as their avatar. It sits next to the battlefield and never leaves the game.
  • Players start at 24 life with a normal 7-card hand.
  • Decide who goes first however you like. The first player usually skips their first draw.

The Momir Vig ability

Once each turn, at sorcery speed, you may pay {X} and discard a card. If you do, you create a token that's a copy of a random creature card with mana value X. Lands can still be played and tapped for mana as normal.

The token is a copy of the creature card with that mana value, so it has the card's abilities, power, and toughness. It does not have haste unless the creature card itself has it.

Using this site

  1. Pay X mana and discard a card, as above.
  2. On the Play page, tap the button matching your X.
  3. The site shows a random creature with that mana value. Use that card for your token.
  4. Tap "Another" if you need a different one, for example after a mistake or for the next turn's activation.

What counts as a creature on this site

  • Creatures legal to play in paper Magic.
  • Cards from Un-sets and other joke or playtest cards are excluded.
  • Each creature appears once, regardless of how many times it's been printed.
  • For double-faced cards, the creature must be on the front face, and only the front face is shown.
  • Creature lands such as Dryad Arbor are included.
  • There are no creatures with mana value 14, so that button is missing.
  • Mana value 0 is allowed, so you can pay nothing and still get a result.

Winning

The normal rules of Magic apply: reduce your opponent's life to 0 to win. Because every card in your deck is a land, drawing and discarding is how you decide how big a creature you can afford.
